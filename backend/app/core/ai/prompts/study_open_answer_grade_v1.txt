Marking rules:
1. Judge meaning, not wording. A correct answer in the student's own words, or with a different but valid example, earns full marks. Repeating keywords without showing understanding does not.
2. Compare the student's answer with the reference answer's key ideas. Do not reward claims that are wrong or that contradict the reference, even if they also contain correct keywords.
3. Score each answer from 0 to 100:
   - 85-100: covers the key ideas correctly.
   - 60-84: mostly correct, but at least one important idea is missing or vague.
   - 30-59: partly correct, with major gaps or a significant misunderstanding.
   - 0-29: wrong, off-topic, or too little to show understanding.
4. "feedback" is 1-3 plain sentences addressed to the student. Say what they got right first, then what was missing or mistaken. Do not simply repeat the reference answer.
5. "missing_points" lists the specific key ideas the student left out or got wrong (empty list when nothing is missing).
